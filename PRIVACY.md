# Privacy

Simple FTP Server does not collect, store, sell, or transmit analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, or personal data to the developer.

When you start the server, the app listens for FTP connections on your device and reads or modifies shared-storage files only in response to commands from connected FTP clients. Network traffic goes directly between your device and those clients. FTP traffic is not encrypted.

Settings such as the port, username, password, and anonymous-mode choice are stored locally in the app's private preferences. Uninstalling the app removes those preferences.
